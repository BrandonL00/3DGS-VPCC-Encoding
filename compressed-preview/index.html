These are reconstructions from the actual combined C18.3 + E19 experiment. The camera stays in place when you switch budgets and resets when you choose another scene.

The browser downloads our compressed packages and reconstructs them locally using WebAssembly. Quality scores are averages from the canonical benchmark, not measurements of this browser view.

Artifact provenance & viewing notes

The 64k, 128k, 256k, 512k and 1.024M labels are requested compaction targets; square-grid fitting slightly reduces the final populations. The decoder restores V-PCC anchors, residuals, AVIF attributes and SH codebooks without applying another quantization step. Browser rendering can differ from the paper's GraphDECO renderer.

Scene downloads include decoding metadata; the shared decoder runtime is loaded separately. Decoding currently needs roughly 1 GB of CPU memory in addition to rendering memory. Full original scenes are outside this gallery. Artifact identities are recorded in the manifest.
